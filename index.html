About the symposium

The first ORAI Research Symposium brings together PhD students, prospective students, and faculty working at the interface of OR and AI.

The day features short research talks and two poster sessions led by ORAI trainees and fellows, along with students from across USC and other universities. It ends with lunch and a faculty panel discussion.

The symposium is a chance for current ORAI trainees and fellows to share their work and get to know one another. It is also a chance for students thinking about a PhD to see what research in ORAI looks like and meet the people doing it.

Who should come

  • PhD students at USC or elsewhere working on OR, AI, or both. Come present a poster.
  • Undergraduate and master's students considering a PhD in OR or AI.
  • Faculty interested in OR and AI research.

Registration is free, and lunch is provided. We have limited funds to help students with travel costs.

Call for posters

All students (PhD, master's, or undergraduate), at USC or elsewhere, can submit a poster on work involving operations research, artificial intelligence, or both.

How to submit

  1. Register as soon as you can. Choose "presenting a poster" and give a title, authors, and a short abstract.
  2. Email your poster as a PDF by the deadline below if you want it considered for the poster prize.
Poster PDF deadline: Monday, December 1, 11:59 pm PT
Email to ldimitro@usc.edu with the subject line
ORAI Symposium Poster – [Last name], [Poster title]

Posters sent after the deadline are still welcome at the symposium but won't be considered for the prize.

Poster details

  • Size: up to 36″ × 48″, portrait or landscape.
  • Printing: presenters print and bring their own posters.
  • Prizes: cash prizes for the best poster and a runner-up, awarded during the 1:00 pm session.
  • Sessions: each poster is presented in one of the two sessions. We'll tell you which one.

What is ORAI?

Funded by an NSF National Research Traineeship grant, ORAI is an interdisciplinary PhD certificate program at USC in Operations Research and Artificial Intelligence. It trains students to do research where the two fields meet, and it is the first graduate program of its kind.

ResearchMethods that combine machine learning with optimization, responsible AI (robust, interpretable, private, and fair), and decision-making from data that is scarce, biased, noisy, or shifting.
TrainingInterdisciplinary research, three of four new OR/AI courses, and a seminar with workshops on ethics and entrepreneurship. The certificate adds no courses for CS or ISE PhD students.
Who can joinAny USC PhD student doing OR and AI research for their dissertation. There are no citizenship requirements. U.S. citizens, nationals, and permanent residents are also eligible for ORAI Fellowships.
